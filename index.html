Executive summary

22 posts were published across the period, together generating 27,523 impressions and 1,208 engagements (reactions and comments), an average engagement rate of 4.4%. The average post reached about 1,251 people.

The strongest post was “Leadership lessons at electronica India 2025” (Post 11): 2,565 impressions and 149 reactions, the best of the period on both counts. Personal, story-led posts (Posts 3, 6, 8 and 11) all beat the average reach, and shop-floor stories such as the robotic soldering post (Post 15) converted especially well, at 6.2% engagement.

Growth is coming from beyond the existing network. 58% of impressions came from out-of-network viewers, account impressions rose 153% against the prior 90 days, and followers grew 17% to 1,880. Parallel outreach added 320 targeted connection requests (60+ accepted in the first month), 64 personal DMs to senior decision-makers, and ongoing thoughtful comments on relevant industry posts.

Account-level figures cover the latest 90-day analytics window, so they sit slightly below the sum of all 22 tracked posts, which also includes the earliest posts.

What the data is telling us

What worked

  • Post 11 (electronica India) paired a personal leadership story with a real industry event and delivered the best reach (2,565) and by far the most reactions (149).
  • Personal and story-led posts consistently outperformed the average, led by Posts 6, 8 and 3.
  • Practical shop-floor content engaged well: Post 15 (robotic soldering) reached 6.2% engagement on 1,340 impressions.
  • Framework content drives discussion: Post 5 (20 levers) earned the most comments (9).
  • 58% of impressions came from out-of-network viewers, and the audience skews towards electronics and appliance manufacturers.

What to adjust

  • Reach slipped on the latest few posts (Posts 19 to 21 sit between 492 and 545 impressions), so stronger opening lines and personal anchors are needed.
  • The EV shift article (Post 18) reached 2,148 people but converted at only 1.4%. Add a clear takeaway and a question.
  • Comments on posts are light, and a few posts drew none. End each post with one specific question.
  • Macro topics without a personal hook (Posts 2 and 7) had the lowest reach among the earlier posts.
  • Directors, VPs and CXOs make up 17% of viewers; outreach should keep pushing that share up.

Plan for the next period

  1. Lead with personal stories. Keep four to five posts that pair a short honest reflection with a photo or real moment.
  2. Anchor topics to real events. Tie lessons to plant visits, conferences and milestones, as with Post 11.
  3. Keep one framework post and one article. Use them for depth, and finish both with a direct question.
  4. Strengthen opening lines. Test sharper hooks on the post types whose reach has slipped.
  5. Convert connections into conversations. Keep the commenting routine going and move more of the 64 DMs into nurtured relationships.
